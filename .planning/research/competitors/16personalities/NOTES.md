# 16Personalities pricing & packaging teardown

> **Project-wide reference.** Gabriel chose 16Personalities (NERIS Analytics) as the pricing/packaging *and* product-UX model to mirror. Source PDFs in this folder, captured 2026-05-30 from https://www.16personalities.com. They are the closest *proven* monetization analog to Potential: a free quiz → free-but-locked results → one-time paid unlocks.
>
> **This informs the whole project, not one phase:**
> - **Product UX** — the free-but-locked/blurred results pattern shapes the results page and the freemium tier gate (Phases 2–3 results, Phase 8 tier gate).
> - **Business model** — pricing, credit/"runs" packaging, and conversion mechanics feed the pitch deliverables (Phase 9).
> - **Pitch Q&A** — 16Personalities is the citable proof point that a one-time, quiz-driven report business works at scale.

## The playbook we're mirroring

| 16Personalities mechanic | Source PDF | How Potential adapts it |
|---|---|---|
| **Free results, premium sections locked/blurred** with padlock + "Get the full report to unlock" | `your-profile.pdf` | Free tier shows minimal results (#1 city); deeper sections (full ranked list, financials detail, live-AI, roadmap, visa) shown blurred/locked to drive curiosity → purchase |
| **One-time premium bundle at $29**, 30-day money-back guarantee, AI feature as the hook (5 AI career mentors) | `premium-career-suite.pdf` | Validates one-time $29.99 Premium tier + live-AI/concierge as the premium hook + money-back guarantee as a conversion lever |
| **Credit/report pricing**: $9 per report, pricing calculator ("how many reports?"), "credits never expire," "no commitment or subscription required," bulk discounts | `reports-for-pros.pdf` | Direct analog for the "runs" model: Basic = 1 run, Mid = 3 runs, Premium = unlimited. Frame runs as credits that never expire, no subscription |
| **Subscription reserved for B2B/Teams**: $9/mo per seat, Monthly/Quarterly/Yearly (save 50% yearly), "stop anytime" | `teams.pdf` | Confirms recurring revenue belongs to the *future B2B* line, not the individual consumer — matches our "consumer is one-time, recurring comes from B2B + affiliate" decision |
| **Trust/conversion mechanics**: testimonials (with "reward for feedback, positive or negative" disclosure), "MOST POPULAR" badge on anchor tier, extensive grouped FAQ, "View Sample Report" everywhere, "SAVE 70% vs individual" bundling | all PDFs | Adopt money-back guarantee, "most popular" badge on Mid, sample-report transparency, and FAQ as Q&A-bank seed |

## Pricing facts (citable analogs)
- 16Personalities Premium Career Suite: **$29 one-time**, 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Reports for Pros: **$9 per report credit**, credits never expire, no subscription, volume pricing.
- Teams (B2B): **$9/month per seat**, save 50% annually, no minimum contract.

## Why this matters for the pitch
16Personalities is a large, profitable, well-known consumer assessment business built on *exactly* this funnel (free quiz → free-but-locked results → one-time unlock). Citing it answers the "will people actually pay one-time for a quiz-driven report?" challenge with a real, recognizable proof point — and shows the subscription-free choice is deliberate, not naive.
